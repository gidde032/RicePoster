Help

Quick guidance for confident, safe posting.

Before you post

Simple checks that save time and protect your accounts.
  • Media is relevant, vertical, and high quality.
  • Captions are accurate and on-brand.
  • Each slot uses unique media.
  • Sessions show green for the platforms you intend to post to.
  • Spacing between posts is appropriate.

Caption generation

How captions are created.
  • The AI drafts a caption from the frame it is shown plus your topic.
  • Captions reflect scene details and intent.
  • Choose a style per slot; Regenerate or Undo as needed.

Scheduling & pacing

Pacing protects reach and account health.
  • Schedule one batch at a time to fire at a chosen local time.
  • Posts within a run are spaced automatically between accounts.
  • A scheduled batch posts unattended — review captions first.

Result meanings

What each result status means.
Confirmed Posted and confirmed on the platform.
Unconfirmed Submitted but not confirmed — check before reposting.
Failed Could not post. See the error reason.
Skipped Not attempted (no caption or no session).

Recovery & retained evidence

If something goes wrong, follow these steps.
  • Check any Unconfirmed result on the platform before reposting.
  • For a Failed result, read the error reason, fix, then retry.
  • Wait the pacing interval before reposting to avoid rate limits.
  • Interrupted batches and their media are retained as honest evidence in Queue.

Real-account safety

This tool posts to live Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • Post All and Confirm Schedule act on real accounts immediately.
  • Review every caption and the target slot before posting.
  • Retained media and captions are kept locally for verification only.
